# Interview Talking Points

- Replaces fragmented roster channels with one controlled source of truth.
- Traces stakeholders and stories into rules, API, schema, and tests.
- Keeps HTTP thin and business rules in testable services.
- Uses soft deactivation and restrictive foreign keys for history.
- Enforces RBAC and ownership on the server.
- Keeps recurring availability and HH:mm values simple for V1; exceptions and complex award/time rules remain V2.
- Phased delivery prioritises scheduling correctness over dashboard polish.
