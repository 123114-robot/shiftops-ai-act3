# Project Overview

ShiftFlow centralises weekly rosters, availability, and leave for shift-based SMEs. V1 serves managers and employees through a React client and REST API. Its portfolio focus is traceability from requirements through rules, tests, implementation, and review.

Success means managers maintain employees and valid rosters while employees maintain availability, request leave, and view personal schedules. Attendance, payroll, notifications, overtime, award interpretation, and swaps are V2.
