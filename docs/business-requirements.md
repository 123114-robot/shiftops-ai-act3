# Business Requirements

- BRQ-01: authenticated manager and employee access.
- BRQ-02: preserve historical rosters through soft deactivation.
- BRQ-03: record recurring weekly availability.
- BRQ-04: maintain dated shifts with optional assignment.
- BRQ-05: reject invalid, overlapping, unavailable, inactive, or approved-leave assignments.
- BRQ-06: support leave requests and manager decisions.
- BRQ-07: provide manager roster and personal schedule views.
- BRQ-08: enforce permissions and personal-data boundaries server-side.
